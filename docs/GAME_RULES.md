# Game rules

Every player begins inside a small circular territory and moves continuously.

- Leaving owned land creates an exposed trail.
- Returning to owned land closes the path and captures the enclosed region.
- Captured enemy cells change ownership.
- Crossing another player's exposed trail eliminates that player.
- Players may cross their own trail.
- Crossing the circular arena boundary eliminates the player.
- Eliminated players lose their territory and respawn after a short delay.
- The leaderboard ranks active players by percentage of the claimable arena, then cuts.

The arena starts when the first human joins, includes twelve bots, and is disposed when the last human leaves.
